### About me

I am a political scientist specialising in gender and political behaviour, with a particular focus on climate and environmental politics. I am a Postdoctoral Researcher on the ERC-funded GREENLOSS project, led by Diane Bolet, at Sciences Po, and hold a PhD in Political and Social Sciences from the European University Institute (EUI).

My research examines how and why political parties, policy issues and political causes become gendered, and how these gendered associations shape political attitudes and behaviour. A central focus of my work is the green gender gap: why women and men differ in their support for green parties and climate policies.

Methodologically, I specialise in quantitative research, using survey data, experimental methods and computational social science. My research has received media coverage from NRK, BBC Radio 4, The European Correspondent and Aftenposten.
